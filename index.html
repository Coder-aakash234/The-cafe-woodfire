Our Story

Where every dish is crafted with passion

Tucked away in the bustling Sector 2 market of Ballabgarh, Faridabad, The Cafe Woodfire is a hidden gem for those who appreciate food made with heart. We are a pure vegetarian cafe dedicated to serving up comfort food that feels like a warm hug.

Inspired by the rustic charm of wood-fired cooking, our menu features a delightful range of burgers, pizzas, pastas, creamy momos, and classic Indian-Chinese favorites. From our famous Aloo Patty Burger to the zesty Honey Chilli Potato, every item is prepared fresh to order.

Whether you're looking for a quick bite or a relaxed meal with friends, our vibrant atmosphere and friendly service promise a memorable dining experience. We're proud to be a local favorite for quality food at pocket-friendly prices.
